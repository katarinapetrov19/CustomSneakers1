Mail Club

40x50 mm artwork

$15.00 per month

Shipping calculated at checkout

A tiny envelope of joy delivered to your mailbox every month. Mail Club is a slow, cozy little tradition, a moment to step away from screens, open something made just for you, and collect a little piece of art each month.

Every month you'll receive:

  • 1 exclusive A6 art print
  • 1 sticker
  • 1 surprise extra (mini print, colouring sheet, or another flat treat!)
  • A letter from me with tiny stories, doodles, and thoughts from my month

All designs are created especially for Mail Club — little artworks you won't find in my shop. It's perfect if you love cute stationery, monthly surprises, or just want something more heartfelt than the internet's chaos. Think of it as a mini adventure that arrives right to your mailbox.

Sign-ups close on the 15th at 11:59 PM EST. Orders after that date begin the following month. Mail goes out during the final week of each month :)

FAQ ♡

How does the subscription work?

You'll be billed monthly and your envelope ships at the end of each month. You can pause, skip, or cancel anytime through your account. It's super simple.

How do I update my address if I move?

After you place an order, you'll get an Order Confirmation Email. Click "Manage Your Subscription" (under "View your order") and log in using the same email you checked out with. Try to update your address before the 20th, just to be safe!

Can I buy this as a gift?

Yes! Just enter their shipping address at checkout. You can add a small gift note too.

Will the art repeat?

Nope! Each month has a totally different theme, feeling, and design. I love experimenting, so there's always something new.

What if my mail gets lost?

If you don't receive it within 2–3 weeks (domestic) or 4–6 weeks (international), send me an email and I'll help sort it out ♡

Can I try just one month?

Absolutely! You can purchase a single month without subscribing long-term.
